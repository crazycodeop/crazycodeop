### 👨‍💻 Executive Summary

I am a **Full-Stack & Systems Engineer** passionate about building robust, high-performance web applications, distributed AI platforms, and reactive mobile experiences. With a rigorous foundation in **Data Structures, Low-Level Design (LLD), and System Architecture**, I specialize in transforming complex product requirements into clean, scalable, production-grade software.

* 🔭 **Currently Building:** Enterprise-grade Document Intelligence & RAG platforms and visual workflow orchestration systems.
* 🧠 **Core Competencies:** Next.js & React ecosystem, Python & FastAPI microservices, Flutter cross-platform architecture, and vector embeddings (pgvector).
* ⚡ **Engineering Philosophy:** Write clean, modular, testable code; design for scale from day one; eliminate bottlenecks before they occur.
* 🎯 **Available For:** Full-Stack Engineer, Backend Engineer, and Frontend / Mobile Engineer opportunities.
